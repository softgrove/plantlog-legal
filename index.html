Privacy Policy

PlantLog is built to keep your data private. Everything you log — your plants, watering and care events, observations, and notes — is stored only on your device using Apple's on-device storage and is never sent to us.

  • To improve the app, PlantLog sends only anonymous usage statistics (for example, which screens and features are used) with a random installation identifier. They never include your entries or any personal information, and are never sold or used for advertising or tracking.
  • No account or sign-up is required.
  • PlantLog contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details; Apple handles billing under its own privacy policy.
  • Deleting the app removes its on-device data.
